# Perth mountain-bike / BMX jumps and pump tracks

Research checked: **10 November 2026** (Australia/Perth).

Scope: Perth metropolitan area and nearby Perth Hills, with 20 locations covering dedicated jump parks, pump tracks and selected MTB jump destinations. This is a practical shortlist, not an exhaustive inventory of neighbourhood tracks. BMX race circuits are identified separately from freestyle dirt jumps.

## How to read the evidence

- **Both** means a pump track and identifiable jumps or jump trails. Rollers alone are not treated as a dedicated jump line.
- **Not confirmed** means the sources did not establish that feature; it does not prove its absence.
- **Undated listing** means a council/operator page was available when checked, but its facility information has no clear update date. It is not a November 2026 inspection or guarantee of today's riding conditions.
- Dates below refer to the actual article, event or report where identifiable. Search-engine crawl dates and misleading “published recently” labels on old council PDFs are not treated as fresh evidence.
- **User consensus is limited.** Publicly accessible rider discussions were sparse and often several years old. Family-guide opinions, council promotion and a builder's claims are labelled accordingly. No venue has enough recent, independent rider evidence here to justify a confident 2026 quality ranking.

## Where I would start

These are recommendations inferred from the documented facilities and limited feedback, rather than a league table of user ratings.

1. **For a dedicated BMX / dirt-jump progression session: John Dunn.** The combination of multiple pump and jump options offers room to progress. The clearest first-hand rider report found praises the consistency of the take-offs and the ability to move onto larger lines, although it dates to approximately 2022. [Rider discussion][john-rider] · [Builder description][john-builder]
2. **For learning repeatable MTB jumps: Dianella.** RAC describes three graded lines of tabletop jumps added in November 2023, providing a useful way to repeat the same features. [RAC assessment][rac]
3. **For a north-of-river mixed-ability outing: Shepherds Bush.** The combination of a pump/jump facility and a separate children's road-skills area gives it broader appeal. Whitfords West and Duncraig are newer alternatives, but the independent feedback collected here is weaker. [Council assessment][kingsley-assessment] · [Whitfords opening][whitfords] · [Duncraig project][duncraig]
4. **For MTB jump flow with some descent: Booyeembara.** Its two roughly 400 m downhill trails offer a different session from a compact BMX jump park. The hard treated surface is part of its fast character. [Official visitor information][boo] · [Riding assessment][rac]
5. **For pump, jumps and flow trails in one southern venue: Point Walter.** The council confirms all three elements. This recommendation rests on the facility mix; I did not find enough independent reviews to call it better than John Dunn or Boo. [City of Melville][melville]
6. **For a larger MTB outing with jump practice: the Goat Farm.** DBCA confirms dedicated jump trails and a skills park. It also notes little shade and no drinking water, which matters when choosing a summer session. [DBCA][goat]

## Location and access details that matter

**The two Bayswater venues are different.** Wotton Street in Embleton is the skatepark-associated jump venue; Katanning Street / Claughton Reserve is the riverside MTB facility under Mooro-Beelo Bridge. Older Wotton references can also predate its relocation. The riverside facility is for daytime riding: council says its installed lights are for security. [Bayswater directory][bayswater] · [Replacement jump-park description][bayswater-builder]

**Manning Bike Track is in Karawara.** Do not confuse George Burnett Park with Manning Park in the Cockburn area. The February 2025 opening notice gives the correct venue. [Opening notice][manning]

**Malabar has access hours and club use.** Council lists public access as 7 am–6 pm daily, with the BMX circuit closing earlier for club training. Confirm the day's arrangements before travelling. [Cockburn directory][cockburn]

**Hamilton Hill / Dixon Reserve is excluded from the riding shortlist.** The council page checked for this research explicitly says the pump track is closed until further notice for unplanned maintenance. [Closure notice in council directory][cockburn]

**Not all “jumps” mean the same thing.** Dianella's reported tabletop progression lines, John Dunn's larger jump options, Booyeembara's downhill trails and Malabar's race circuit are different experiences. Use the table's type column when choosing a destination. [Dianella and Boo assessment][rac] · [John Dunn description][john-dunn] · [Malabar listing][cockburn]

## What the quality evidence does — and does not — tell us

The clearest first-hand quality observation collected is the older John Dunn rider account. Other local discussion mostly names tracks, rather than assessing their surfaces, maintenance or jump design. For example, the Perth pump-track thread suggests Hester Park and Malabar, but a mention is not the same as an endorsement backed by repeated visits. [John Dunn account][john-rider] · [Perth discussion][perth-pumps]

The February 2026 family roundup is useful recent editorial coverage, particularly for families, but its publication date does not prove a 2026 visit to every track. Likewise, a council directory establishes a facility's listed features more reliably than its current surface condition. [Family roundup][family-roundup]

No Google star scores are reproduced: a whole reserve's score can reflect playgrounds, walking, dogs or picnics rather than the riding. For example, the accessible Booyeembara review aggregator is explicitly for the entire park. [Whole-park listing][boo-reviews]

This research did not verify same-day openings or inspect sites. The biggest remaining gap is recent rider reporting on maintenance, crowding and progression between jump sizes. “No consensus found” should be read as an evidence gap, not a negative verdict.
