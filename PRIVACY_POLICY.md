# Privacy Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to BODAQS Desktop and the official BODAQS release artifacts
published from the [BODAQS GitHub repository](https://github.com/benconnor1972/BODAQS).

## Local data processing

BODAQS Desktop is designed to process data locally. It does not transfer user
information to BODAQS-operated network services.

## Network-based functions

When a user configures or requests Wi-Fi logger discovery, download, or other
network-based functions, BODAQS Desktop communicates with the BODAQS logger or
network host selected or operated by that user. These functions may use the
local network, including local-network discovery, only to provide the requested
device connection and data-transfer functionality.

## Third parties

The privacy practices of third-party software, services, or hardware used with
BODAQS are governed by their respective policies and terms.

## Contact

For a privacy question or concern about BODAQS, contact the project maintainer
through the repository.
