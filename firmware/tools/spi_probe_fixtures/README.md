# Independent analyzer examples

These literal JSONL captures are worked host fixtures, not device measurements
or captures generated by the firmware emitter. Historical `mixed.jsonl` remains
unchanged. `round2-mixed.jsonl` adds the frozen v2 diagnostic schema.

The v2 clean example has 200 successful decode calls and 150 accepted protocol
responses: successful decoding alone does not establish response acceptance.
Participant successes are 5 unknown plus 195 after CS10. Four expected-checksum
calls total 28 us. Its page2 example crosses uint32 wrap: process calls advance
from 4294967290 to144 (150 calls), process total from4294967280 to32 (48 us),
retirement total from4294967290 to6 (12 us), and publication total from
4294967290 to24 (30 us). Boot process maxima80/90 remain endpoints.

`round2-failures.jsonl` has ten slots: one transfer failure, six decode successes,
one magic rejection and two CRC rejections. Two full replies are retained and
one rejection is omitted. Unknown previous participant has one success and one
failure; CS10 has five successes and two failures. These account for all nine
decode calls independently of the first-sample selection.

The all-zero reply fails magic. The second reply starts with magic53/50,
version1, response opcode82, case203, object1 and profile24 (64 bytes, 8 MHz,
four nodes); its deliberately zero frame CRC is invalid. The analyzer preserves
reported reasons and bytes, rather than executing a replacement firmware decode.
The first sample asserts CS at timestamp0, releases at8, and has no known
preceding operation. The second follows a release at4294967290, asserts at0,
and releases at12: a six-microsecond preceding gap and12 us software ownership.
Sequence0 and timestamp0 are valid observations.

Tests mutate these literals through the public CLI to reject inconsistent
counts, missing source identity, malformed hex, impossible request identities,
unbounded samples, invalid timing and missing measurement states. No physical
SCK, electrical reliability, ADC acquisition rate or drain capacity is inferred.
