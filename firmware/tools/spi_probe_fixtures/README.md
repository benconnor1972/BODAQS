# Independent analyzer examples

`mixed.jsonl`, `round2-mixed.jsonl` and `round2-failures.jsonl` are worked host
fixtures, not device measurements or captures generated by the firmware emitter.
Historical `mixed.jsonl` remains unchanged. `round2-mixed.jsonl` adds the frozen
v2 diagnostic schema.

The v2 clean example has 200 successful decode calls and 150 accepted protocol
responses: successful decoding alone does not establish response acceptance.
Participant successes are 5 unknown plus 195 after CS10. Four expected-checksum
calls total 28 us. Its page2 example crosses uint32 wrap: process calls advance
from 4294967290 to 144 (150 calls), process total from 4294967280 to 32 (48 us),
retirement total from 4294967290 to 6 (12 us), and publication total from
4294967290 to 24 (30 us). Boot process maxima 80/90 remain endpoints.

`round2-failures.jsonl` has ten slots: one transfer failure, six decode successes,
one magic rejection and two CRC rejections. Two full replies are retained and
one rejection is omitted. Unknown previous participant has one success and one
failure; CS10 has five successes and two failures. These account for all nine
decode calls independently of the first-sample selection.

The all-zero reply fails magic. The second reply starts with magic 53/50,
version 1, response opcode 0x82, case 203, object 1 and profile 24 (64 bytes,
8 MHz, four nodes). Its deliberately zero frame CRC is invalid: an independent
stdlib `zlib.crc32` check gives 0x5b8bfdf7. The analyzer preserves
reported reasons and bytes, rather than executing a replacement firmware decode.
The first sample asserts CS at timestamp 0, releases at 8, and has no known
preceding operation. The second follows a release at 4294967290, asserts at 0,
and releases at 12: a six-microsecond preceding gap and 12 us software ownership.
Sequence 0 and timestamp 0 are valid observations.

Tests mutate these literals through the public CLI to reject inconsistent
counts, missing source identity, malformed hex, impossible request identities,
unbounded samples, invalid timing and missing measurement states. No physical
SCK, electrical reliability, ADC acquisition rate or drain capacity is inferred.

`round2-emitted-switch.jsonl` is actual JSON emitted by D2's compiled C++ public
command host harness, extracted as the case 12 line from external
`round2/logger/public-cases.jsonl` (capture SHA256
283990df52e1e77f54f8813e23b0ab58b974dee43ce5eb91154dadaf9c6a3bec).
The JSON line is preserved with an LF terminator. Its emitted logger identity is
`spi-probe-v2-38648e1a11d9-6c700f8c195d33742441`. It covers actual serialization
of SPI participant -2 for unselected clocks. This is host evidence, not a device
timing or ADC-health observation. The case includes a deliberate command error,
and the report correctly retains failed eligibility.
# Round8 fixtures

`round8-baseline/pipeline/stop.jsonl` are emitted development host captures from
the actual logger wrapper with table CRC. Clocks, GPIO, SPI, RTOS and peer reply
delay are simulated. They exercise cadence/CRC reporting and analyzer rejection;
their source identities precede the final committed target images. They are not
physical captures or admission evidence. Final target identities and offline
validation captures are retained with the sealed round8 bundle.
